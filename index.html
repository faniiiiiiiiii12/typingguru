Free online typing test

This typing speed test measures your words per minute (WPM) and accuracy using random common English words. Choose a 15 second, 30 second, 1 minute, 2 minute, 3 minute or 5 minute test, or switch to the harder mode with capital letters, numbers and punctuation.

How the test works

  1. Click the text box and begin typing. The timer starts with your first key.
  2. Correct letters turn green, mistakes turn red. Use Backspace to fix errors.
  3. When time is up you see your WPM, raw WPM, accuracy and a graph of your speed.

How to type faster

  • Learn touch typing: keep your fingers on the home row (A S D F and J K L ;) and avoid looking at the keyboard.
  • Focus on accuracy first. Speed comes naturally once mistakes stop slowing you down.
  • Practise 10 to 15 minutes every day instead of one long session a week.
  • Keep a steady rhythm. Do not rush and do not pause between words.
  • Sit upright, relax your shoulders and keep your wrists level.

Frequently asked questions

How is typing speed (WPM) calculated?

WPM means words per minute. One word is counted as 5 characters, including spaces. Your WPM is the number of correctly typed characters divided by 5, divided by the minutes taken. Mistakes reduce your score.

What is a good typing speed?

Most people type between 38 and 45 WPM. Above 60 WPM is considered fast, and above 80 WPM is professional level. For office and data entry jobs, 40 to 60 WPM with high accuracy is usually enough.

What is the difference between WPM and raw WPM?

Raw WPM counts every character you typed, right or wrong. WPM counts only the correct characters, so it is the fairer measure of your real speed.

Is my typing saved or shared?

No. The test runs entirely in your browser. Your best score is stored only on your own device.
